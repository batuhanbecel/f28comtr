# Product Context

## Why This Project Exists
f/2.8 Production is a professional photography portfolio website designed to showcase the work of multiple photographers and AI-based creative projects. The name "f/2.8" refers to a common aperture setting in photography, representing professional-grade equipment and expertise.

## Problems It Solves
1. **Portfolio Showcase**: Provides a centralized platform for multiple photographers to display their work
2. **Modern Presentation**: Offers a contemporary, visually appealing way to present photography
3. **AI Integration**: Bridges traditional photography with AI-generated creative work
4. **Professional Image**: Creates a polished, professional online presence for the production company
5. **Easy Navigation**: Intuitive interface for clients to browse different photographers and styles

## How It Should Work

### User Journey - Client/Visitor
1. **Landing**: Arrives at split-screen homepage
   - Sees two clear options: PRODUCTION or AI BASED
   - Understands the dual nature of the business immediately
2. **Production Path**:
   - Clicks PRODUCTION → sees photographer showcase sections
   - Scrolls through parallax sections featuring different photographers
   - Clicks on a photographer → views their full portfolio in masonry grid
   - Can navigate to other photographers or return home
3. **AI-Based Path**:
   - Clicks AI BASED → sees masonry grid of AI-generated work
   - Browses creative AI projects
   - Clean, distraction-free viewing experience
4. **About/Contact**:
   - Accesses via hamburger menu
   - Finds company information and contact details
   - Views services, partners, and clients

### User Journey - Photographer
1. Their work is featured in a dedicated portfolio section
2. Professional presentation with optimized image loading
3. Masonry grid layout showcases variety of work
4. Individual portfolio page accessible via direct link

## User Experience Goals

### Visual Design
- **Clean & Modern**: Minimalist aesthetic that puts photography first
- **Smooth Animations**: Subtle, professional transitions that enhance rather than distract
- **Typography-First**: Clear hierarchy with bold, impactful headings
- **Dark Theme**: Black background that makes images pop
- **Responsive**: Seamless experience across all device sizes

### Interaction Design
- **Intuitive Navigation**: Clear paths to all content
- **Fast Loading**: Optimized images that load quickly
- **Smooth Scrolling**: Parallax effects that feel natural
- **Touch-Friendly**: Mobile-optimized interactions
- **Accessible**: Keyboard navigation and screen reader support

### Content Strategy
- **Visual-First**: Images are the hero, text is minimal
- **Clear Categorization**: Production vs AI-based work clearly separated
- **Photographer Identity**: Each photographer has their own space
- **Professional Tone**: Sophisticated, high-end presentation
